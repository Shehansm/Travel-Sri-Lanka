Taste of Serendib

Explore the rich, spicy, and vibrant culinary heritage of Sri Lanka. An explosion of flavor in every bite.

The Staple

Rice and Curry

The national dish of Sri Lanka. It isn't just one curry, but a magnificent feast of rice accompanied by a meat or fish curry, several vegetable curries, sambals, and crispy papadam.

  • Must Try: Black Pork Curry and Dhal (Lentil) Curry
  • Support Local: Eat at local 'Bath Kades' (Rice shops) to directly support small family businesses.
Street Food

Kottu & Hoppers

Listen for the rhythmic clanking of metal blades on hot iron skillets; that’s the sound of Kottu Roti being made. It's a deliciously spicy mix of chopped flatbread, vegetables, egg, and meat.

Don't miss Appa (Hoppers), bowl-shaped pancakes made from fermented rice flour and coconut milk, often eaten with a spicy onion sambal (Lunu Miris).

  • Best Time: Kottu is strictly a dinner or late-night street food.
