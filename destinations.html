Places to Visit

From ancient ruins hidden in the jungle to mist-covered tea plantations, explore the best of Sri Lanka.

Cultural Triangle

Sigiriya Rock Fortress

Rising almost 200 meters from the central plains, Sigiriya is the most dramatic sight in Sri Lanka. This ruined palace features stunning water gardens, ancient frescoes, and a pair of colossal lion's paws carved into the rock.

  • Best time: Early morning to avoid heat
  • Difficulty: Moderate (approx. 1200 steps)
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site

South Coast

Mirissa & The South Coast

Relax on the golden sands of Mirissa, famous for its palm-fringed beaches and incredible whale-watching opportunities. Experience the relaxed surf culture and vibrant marine life.

  • Highlights: Blue Whale watching excursions
  • Vibe: Chill, surfing, lively evenings
  • Sustainable travel tip: Choose ethical whale watching operators

Hill Country

Nuwara Eliya & Tea Country

Often referred to as 'Little England', the hill country features carpet-like tea plantations, roaring waterfalls, and cool, misty climates perfect for scenic train rides.

  • Must do: The Kandy to Ella train ride
  • Local support: Visit farm-direct tea factories
  • Climate: Cool (10°C - 20°C)
