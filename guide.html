Essential Travel Guide

Everything you need to know to plan the perfect trip to Sri Lanka: visa process, seasonal weather, and transportation.

Visa Process

Most nationalities require an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) prior to arrival. It is a simple online process.

  • Valid for 30 days
  • Apply online via official portal
  • Passport must be valid for 6 months
Official ETA Portal →

Best Time to Travel

Sri Lanka is a year-round destination, but the best time depends on which coast you want to visit due to two monsoons.

  • West & South Coasts: Dec to March
  • East Coast: May to September
  • Hill Country: Dec to March (cool and dry)

How to Travel

Getting around is part of the adventure. There are multiple ways to explore the island.

  • Trains: Scenic and cheap (especially Kandy to Ella)
  • Tuk-tuks: Perfect for short coastal trips. Remember to negotiate!
  • Buses: Extensive network, very affordable but can be crowded.
  • Private Driver: Best for comfort and flexibility.
